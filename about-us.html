About DonaJyoti Dental Clinic

Welcome to DonaJyoti Dental Clinic, your destination for comprehensive dental care in Anjad, Madhya Pradesh.

Led by Dr. Donajyoti Sarkar — BDS (Gold Medalist), Orthodontics, our clinic is committed to promoting healthy smiles and better oral health through a wide range of dental treatments. We offer general, preventive, restorative, orthodontic, cosmetic, and pediatric dental services, including dental check-ups, root canal treatment, teeth cleaning, tooth extraction, dental implants, braces, crowns and bridges, dentures, and teeth whitening.

Our clinic focuses on understanding individual patient needs and providing appropriate dental care with professional attention. With specialist doctor facilities and convenient opening hours throughout the week, we aim to make dental care accessible to our community.
